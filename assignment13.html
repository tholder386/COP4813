How to Play TAP OUT!!

Inspired by Mike Tyson's Punch-Out!!, this game teaches the positional hierarchy of Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu:

  • Standing & Clinch: Initiate a clinch to roll for a takedown. You will land in either Top Half Guard (+2 pts) or Bottom Full Guard.
  • Positional Hierarchy: Advance from Full Guard → Half Guard → Mount → Back Control (or sweep from bottom to top) to break your opponent's posture.
  • Telegraphed Counters: Watch the opponent! When they flash RED to pass or sweep, hit COUNTER [SPACE] before the timer bar empties to reverse them (including taking their back)!
  • Submissions: Once opponent posture drops below 45%, position-specific submissions unlock (Armbar, Guillotine, Toe Hold, Americana, Kimura, Rear Naked Choke).

Performance Scoring: Measured by IBJJF positional points, move efficiency, and submission finish time.
